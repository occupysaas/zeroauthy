# OpenID conformance dry-run triage (2026-10)

Plan 12, Task 2. One row per failed, warning, skipped or review module, per plan. Classes (design §5):
- **bug:** zeroauthy does neither what Auth0 does nor what the spec says;
- **config:** a harness or suite-configuration problem (fixed in the harness, not listed in the final run);
- **parity conflict:** zeroauthy deliberately follows documented or observed Auth0 behaviour;
- **unclear:** it is not known what Auth0 does;
- **feature gap:** zeroauthy lacks the feature.

Parity conflicts, unclear rows and feature gaps go to the maintainer one at a time (Task 2, Step 6). Bug rows need no ruling. No product code was changed for this triage.
